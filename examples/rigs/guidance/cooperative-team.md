# Working with coworkers

Keep your native plan, task graph, tools, and subagents. Branchyard coordinates
work between harness sessions. You do not need to copy your internal task
graph into Branchyard.

Agree on the output contract and shared interfaces before parallel work.
Give coding coworkers separate owned paths. Give researchers separate
questions and a result schema. Branchyard child limits apply to Branchyard
branches, not to the harness's native subagents. Your harness's own permissions
and limits still apply. Dollar limits depend on reported usage and cannot
provide a hard spending guarantee when the harness reports no cost.

Keep working while your native tasks or tools are active. A managed lead may
end its turn to await Branchyard children when its own native work is finished.
An external lead keeps its existing session and uses explicit waits or status
commands. Operator commands do not adopt or restart that session.

Review evidence before using a result. A valid JSON shape does not establish
that a finding is true. Handoffs carry a settled result and selected immutable
artifacts to an existing recipient's next turn. A handoff never starts that turn.
