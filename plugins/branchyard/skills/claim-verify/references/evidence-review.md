# Evidence appraisal

Adapted from K-Dense's scientific-critical-thinking skill. Changes: a short cross-domain evidence review guide; clinical frameworks and optional service-backed figures omitted.

Define the claim's population, outcome, period and intended scope. Distinguish descriptive observations, predictions and causal conclusions. Identify the independent unit behind any count or comparison.

Locate the actual result before judging it: exact source location, numerator, denominator, uncertainty, method and relevant supplementary evidence. Missing reporting and evidence that a procedure did not occur are different observations.

Check selection effects, confounding, measurement, dependence and multiple comparisons where relevant. A fitted model or a matching quotation alone cannot establish a causal effect.

Record each concern as source location, observation, consequence for the claim, uncertainty and a feasible remedy. Keep supported conclusions, assumptions and recommendations separate. Explain conflicting evidence rather than resolving it by counting citations.
